portfolio_optimizer/

Multi-Objective Evolutionary Portfolio Optimisation

Sharpe 1.055 ▲

A multi-objective evolutionary framework across S&P 500 and LSE data (2015–2024), optimizing skewness and kurtosis alongside return and risk via NSGA-III reference-direction niching. Ledoit-Wolf shrinkage and RMT denoising keep the covariance estimates honest at high dimensions, and a parametric Student's-t CVaR (via moment matching) shows heavy-tail risk optimization holds up against historical simulation.

Dual-backend, RLS-secured
